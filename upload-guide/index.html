Add FPV music

This is a separate, explicitly selected collection. Unknown licence labels stay unknown; publication grants no open licence or game-default approval.

  1. Open Add music and select MP3 files or stable hosted MP3 URLs.
  2. Enter artist, title, source, styles, collections and exact licence; confirm recording-specific publication rights.
  3. Download the prepared .rlintake package.
  4. From your clean revealline-soundtracks-fpv checkout, run:
node intake/add-music.mjs "/absolute/path/package.rlintake" --open-pr

The command uploads exact hash-addressed audio, opens a PR and checks metadata. Audio becomes public before catalogue review. Merge only after checks and review; Pages deploys automatically.
